Help

Make adjustments while stationary. Detection and alerts continue while Help is open during a ride.

Start a ride

Mount your phone securely and point the camera toward the traffic you want to watch. Choose Ahead or Behind, then Start ride and allow camera access.

Change watch direction

Open Menu during a ride and choose Front or Rear. Check that the camera is aimed correctly after switching.

Border glow and distance meter

The border changes from lime to yellow to red as the warning level increases. The right-side scale shows estimated vehicle distances, with closer vehicles nearer the bottom. No marker does not mean the road is clear. Distances are approximate.

Sound and camera view

Sound controls audio alerts. Camera shows or hides the live image; detection continues with the image hidden. Vibration support depends on your browser and device.

Pause or end

Pause stops detection and new alerts but leaves the camera on. Resume restarts detection. Menu → End ride stops the camera and detection and returns to the home screen. Export any timing recording before ending; it is not saved automatically.

Settings and diagnostics

Detection settings on the home screen select the model for the next ride. Experimental models are unvalidated on iPhone. During a ride, Menu provides distance-zone tuning, Diagnostics, and timing exports.

If detection stops

Read the on-screen fault message. Check camera permission and the network connection used to download the model. Keep the app visible; switching tabs or locking the screen can interrupt detection.

BikSAFE is a traffic-awareness aid. Keep watching the road and checking mirrors or shoulders.
