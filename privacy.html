BikSAFE Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Camera Use

BikSAFE uses the device camera to detect nearby road users and estimate traffic awareness zones. Camera frames are processed on the device.

Video Storage and Uploads

BikSAFE does not record, store, sell, or upload camera video. If this changes in a future version, the app and this policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Settings

BikSAFE stores local preferences on the device, such as camera mode, distance thresholds, detection mode, and first-run safety acknowledgement.

Diagnostics

The current app does not require an account and does not send personal diagnostics to a server. Crash or performance reporting should only be added after the store disclosures and this policy are updated.

Safety Notice

BikSAFE is a traffic-awareness aid. It is not a substitute for attentive riding, mirrors, shoulder checks, lights, local traffic laws, or safe route choices.
